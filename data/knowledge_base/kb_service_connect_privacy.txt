Service Connect Consent and Location Privacy

Unwanted dealer calls or texts from Service Connect are an opt-in consent issue. Owners can disable dealer notifications in the Toyota app privacy portal, reply STOP, or ask Brand Engagement at 1-800-331-4331. Explain that Safety Connect, Service Connect, and Remote Connect collect different data. Live location is for dispatch and legal process. Law-enforcement location requests generally need a warrant except exigent 1-800-331-4331 cases.
