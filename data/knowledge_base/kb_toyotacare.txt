ToyotaCare Maintenance and Loaner Rules

ToyotaCare covers factory-scheduled maintenance such as oil and filter as specified, tire rotation, multi-point inspection, and fluid inspection. It is valid at participating Toyota dealers using Genuine Parts when available, not only the selling dealer. Extra dealer-recommended services are not automatically complimentary. Overnight warranty repairs that cannot finish the same day may qualify for transportation assistance; convenience overnights do not.
