Toyota Certified Collision Center and ADAS Calibration

After a crash, the owner generally chooses the repair facility even if an insurer recommends another shop. Toyota Certified Collision Centers use processes for Genuine parts, airbags, and Toyota Safety Sense calibration after windshield, bumper, or radar work. Skipping ADAS recalibration can leave pre-collision braking or lane tracing inoperative. Document the claim and locate the nearest TCCC.
