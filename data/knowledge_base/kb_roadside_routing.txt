Toyota Roadside and Brand Engagement Routing

Immobilized, overheating, or red-light highway calls go to Toyota Roadside at 1-800-444-4195, the Toyota app 24/7 button, or the in-vehicle SOS button for Safety Connect. Warranty, dealer disputes, and recalls go to the Brand Engagement Center at 1-800-331-4331 after the vehicle is safe. Destination Assist is not the SOS button. TFS loans and leases go to 1-800-874-8822.
