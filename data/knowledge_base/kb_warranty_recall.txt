Warranty, Goodwill, and Recall Campaign Guide

Typical new-vehicle coverage is 3 years/36,000 miles basic and 5 years/60,000 miles powertrain. If a dealer cannot resolve a warranty concern, Brand Engagement opens a case with VIN, mileage, and dealer name. Safety recalls and service campaigns are free; VIN lookup determines whether the owner may drive or must stop. Stop-sale or do-not-drive bulletins require tow and loaner, not continued commuting.
