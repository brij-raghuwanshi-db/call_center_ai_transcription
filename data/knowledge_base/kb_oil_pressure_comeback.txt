Oil Pressure and Post-Service Come-Back Playbook

A red oil-pressure light means stop immediately and shut the engine off. Driving with low oil pressure can destroy the engine. If the oil light appears after a recent oil change, treat it as a dealer come-back: tow to the servicing Toyota dealer to inspect oil level, drain plug, filter, and specification. Do not send the owner to an independent service center while the oil light is red.
